# Greece Trip 2027 Presentation Draft
**Trip theme:** 2 adults • April 2027 • mix of relaxing and exploring • no lamb • website-ready content for Antigravity / GitHub Pages

---

## Overview

**Trip structure**
- Days 1 to 4: Athens
- Days 5 to 10: Chania, Crete
- Days 11 to 13: Santorini
- Day 14: Athens airport overnight
- Day 15: Fly home

**Travel logic**
- Fly LAX or Ontario to Athens
- Fly Athens to Chania
- Fly or ferry Chania to Santorini depending on schedule
- Fly Santorini to Athens
- Stay at Athens airport hotel the final night to break up travel and protect the international flight home

## Copy Block for Website Intro

This Greece 2027 itinerary is designed for two adults traveling in April with a mix of relaxing and exploring. The trip starts with four nights in Athens for history, neighborhoods, and food, then shifts to six nights in Chania, Crete for depth, scenery, and a private wine and olive oil day, and finishes with three nights in Santorini for caldera views and a slower scenic ending. The return includes one final night at the Athens airport hotel to break up travel and reduce stress before the long flight home.
