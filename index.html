Trip Overview

This Greece 2027 itinerary is designed for four adults traveling in April. The trip starts with three nights in Athens for history, neighborhoods, and food, then shifts to six nights in Chania, Crete for depth, scenery, and a private wine and olive oil day, and finishes with three nights in Santorini for caldera views and a slower scenic ending. The return includes one final night at the Athens airport hotel to break up travel and reduce stress before the long flight home.
